Privacy policy

Object Detection for Android (package es.jrg.objectdetection). Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Object Detection recognises objects in the image from your phone's camera, entirely on your phone. The short version: the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Camera

The app asks for the camera permission to show and analyse what the camera sees. Every frame is processed on your phone and then discarded. Camera images and the detection results are never sent anywhere.

Videos you record

If you press Record, the app saves a video of the camera image with the detection boxes to the Movies/ObjectDetection folder of your phone. The video stays on your phone, under your control, until you delete it. The app never uploads it.

Internet use

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It uses the internet only when you choose Manage models and type the web address of a model file: the app then downloads that file from the address you gave, and from nowhere else. As with any download, the server you choose can see your IP address and the address you requested. The app sends nothing else.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores on your phone only: the models you downloaded, and its settings (the selected model and the confidence setting). Deleting a model in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes them. The app does not back its data up to the cloud.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. The software it is built on (LiteRT, ONNX Runtime, AndroidX) runs on your phone and does not send data off the device through this app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
